Guidance

  • Timing is right. The store is on an upward revenue trajectory (Jan→Dec nearly tripled). Price adjustments during growth periods are absorbed far more easily than during flat or declining phases.
  • Commission framing matters. Lead the team meeting with the groomer win: +$2.50/appointment = ~$102/month per groomer. When groomers see personal upside, they become advocates instead of resistors.
  • Pre-book strategy. Offering existing clients the chance to “lock in current pricing” by pre-booking 2-3 appointments before May 1 accelerates near-term revenue and deepens commitment.
  • Monitor, don't fear. Track weekly appointment volume for 8 weeks post-implementation. If bookings drop more than 5% from the trailing 4-week average, consider a targeted loyalty offer for at-risk segments — but don't preemptively discount.
  • Pair with value. Announce the increase alongside a tangible quality improvement: new products, extended appointment slots, complimentary teeth check, or a loyalty perk. The narrative is “more value” not “higher price.”
